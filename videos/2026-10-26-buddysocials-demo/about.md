# BuddySocials demo

A 40-second vertical walkthrough of the BuddySocials Android app: the widget home, customising it, native post metrics, the shared inbox, goals and the home screen widgets. It closes on "Built on Leo's Social SDK".

## Style

Francesco's design language (oddofrancesco.com/design), adapted to video: black background, white and coral (#D6544B), Chakra Petch Bold uppercase headlines with one coral word, IBM Plex Mono captions (four words at a time, no commas or full stops), square 3px white outlines with a hard white shadow, and mechanical easing.

## Recordings

Screen recordings from the Galaxy Z Fold 8 inner display (release build, demo data), made with `e2e/record-demo.ts`. They have no speech. The status bar is cropped out.
